## What shipped

Each feature has a spec in `docs/superpowers/specs/` and a plan in `docs/superpowers/plans/`, dated
2026-10-02 or 2026-10-03. Every task was implemented by an agent, then spec-reviewed and
quality-reviewed, with a final cross-repo review.

1. **Engine: fractional bar lengths (`ef966ab`).** Half-bar and 1.5-bar stems were silent or looped
   at the wrong length, which was about 1 in 10 Discover rows. Fixed.
2. **Turnarounds**, in both radios.
   - **What happens:** at phrase ends, one move ending on the one:
     - drum drop or low drop;
     - stop, which keeps a melodic row;
     - wash;
     - high-pass lift or low-pass dip;
     - riser.
   - **Where the logic lives:** the shared planner `src/shared/radioTurnaround.ts`.
   - **Controls:** `turnarounds` off / rare / often, `moves` families, and `depth`.
   - **Theory:** spec §0 (Solberg, Iler/Butler).
3. **Turn button**, in both radios and on the phone.
   - **What it does:** a turnaround on demand at the next loop top. The planner chooses, or you tap a
     move chip to pick one.
   - **Keys and phone:** `t` on desktop; `POST /api/turn` from the phone.
4. **Fold mode (Autechre-inspired; v1 then v2).**
   - **What it does:** polymeter. Up to two short rhythmic rows loop at odd lengths against a 4/4
     anchor and realign every 30–120 s. Seeded rules (Autechre hate dice).
   - **Engine:** a lap clock plus a `CycleTable` staged a lap ahead (`stage-cycles` IPC), with 10 ms
     seam fades and crossfades at every change at a top.
   - **v2 added:**
     - re-fold at realignment and rotation;
     - changes every 8–32 bars;
     - drift that scales with bend;
     - the per-row `7 / 16` readout with a phase dot, plus a status line;
     - `bend` / `mismatch` labels;
     - any-text seeds, with a random first seed;
     - a start-screen switch like the visuals switch.
   - **Research:** spec §0 is the Autechre research. §5 of the v1 spec is "as built".
5. **Radio readout**, in both radios.
   - **What it shows:**
     - a status line, e.g. `building ↑ 3 → 5 · next: row 2 → filter in · 6 bars`;
     - a phrase ruler with the armed turnaround;
     - a row label and age;
     - a `next` marker;
     - gesture flash words.
   - **Logic:** `src/shared/radioReadout.ts`. The web's "next" follows the density arc (`4feab3d`);
     the desktop port of that was in progress at handoff (see below).
6. **Faves dial**, in both radios.
   - **What it does:** 0–100, from ignore to favourites only. Each pick is favourites-only with
     probability faves/100; if no favourite fits, it falls back to a normal pick (`no fave fits`).
   - **Replaces:** Discover's `prefer faves` toggle.
   - **Favourites are:**
     - on the desktop, 👍-starred stems;
     - on the web, the visitor's 👍 likes plus hearted stems.
7. **Web radio extras:**
   - the full-mode controls fade only after 5 minutes;
   - the mobile layout wraps instead of overflowing;
   - fold is off by default;
   - the Fluoddity colour band moves at most every 2 minutes (`FLUODDITY_HUE_MIN_GAP_MS`).
8. **Smaller, earlier in the session:**
   - **Link loop folders into IMPORT** (built earlier the same day).
   - **Discover artist mode:** restrictions lifted (`6e1b391`).
   - **Radio menu:** label width fix.

## Open threads (not started)

1. **"Transitions feel early" (investigated, root-caused, not fixed).**
   - **Cause:** mic and line stems (Endlesss `Instrument & 16`, about 35% of stems) sit late in
     their own files: median +7 ms, p90 +25 ms. Measured against each stem's 16th grid; the scripts
     are in this session's scratchpad (gone with it).
   - **Proposed fix:** per-stem onset compensation measured in the background analysis, then played
     that much earlier. No spec yet.
   - **Also found:**
     - Discover and radio never apply re-one; Endlesss stores no offset;
     - gestures are evaluated at the block end, so mid-lap gesture starts can be up to one buffer
       early;
     - Rubber Band R2 smears transients on big slow-downs (R3 `-3` fixes it).

## How this session worked (it worked; reuse it)

- **Pipeline:** brainstorm, then spec, then plan (a planning agent compiles and tests the plan code in
  a scratch copy), then a fresh implementer agent per task, then an independent reviewer per task,
  with fixes looping back, then a final cross-repo review.
- **What review caught:** most real bugs were timing and ordering issues found in review: same-tick
  microtask order, a lap clock vs a renderer lap count, project swaps renaming stems, hold or pause
  desyncing state. Keep reviewers on radio-timing code.
- **Parallel work:** run tasks in parallel only when they touch disjoint files.
- **Shared rules** live in `sssketch/src/shared/`. The web radio imports them via `@shared` (its
  tsconfig has `noUnusedLocals`). Every shared change must keep `ell.ing/radio`'s
  `npm run typecheck` green.
